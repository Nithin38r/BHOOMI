    Batch log

      Safety rules built in

      • Small steps: dose → mix 3 min → measure → repeat. The water's response is learned each step, so no fixed formula is trusted.
      • Hard cap of 600 mL stock solution per batch; if pH is still out of range the batch is rejected and a lab alkalinity test is requested.
      • Only verified water moves to storage; rejected batches drain back.
      • Salinity (EC) and mud cannot be fixed by dosing — only by blending or settling.
      • Choose acid / alkali after a water test; avoid sodium-based alkali (raises the soil's sodium hazard).

      Pond node water quality

      Floating probe: pH, EC (K=1), turbidity, DS18B20 water temperature and an optional DO probe. pH and EC are read one after the other so their electrodes do not interfere. Starts the aerator below 4 mg/L by itself.
